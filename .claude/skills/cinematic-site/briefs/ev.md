# Electric vehicle — "built from the battery out"

## Identity
- **slug** `ev`
- **Niche** EV marque, reservation-driven launch
- **Physics** Reverse explode-view; rigid mechanical assembly
- **Brand names** `VOLTARC` · `AXION`
- **Palette** `--void #06070A` `--magnesium #D6D9DE` `--acid #B6FF3C` `--graphite #22262C` `--white #FFFFFF`
- **Type** Tight geometric sans throughout. Mono for specs. No serif anywhere.

## Scene beats
1. **Opening** — A single cylindrical battery cell, macro, rotating slowly in pure void. Cold rim light. Reads as jewellery, not hardware.
2. **Multiplication** — Hundreds of cells rush in from off-frame and tile themselves into a battery pack. Camera pulls back through the assembly as it forms.
3. **Chassis** — The skateboard platform builds around the pack. Suspension arms unfold like a spider's legs. Motors spin into their mounts.
4. **BLUR-THROUGH — the cladding** — Body panels fly in from every edge at speed, briefly filling the frame completely, then settling onto the frame. Liquid mercury paint flows over the shell and sets.
5. **Ignition** — The headlight signature illuminates. Camera orbits low, catching the light along the body line.
6. **Resolution** — Wide pull-back: the car on a wet salt flat at blue hour, reflection beneath it. It drives out of frame left.

## Ending frame
Empty wet salt flat with tyre trace and receding light — the footer content sits on the empty road. Deliberately anticlimactic; it earns the CTA.

## Products
Two trims (Range / Performance) plus a configurator entry. Reserve with deposit.

## Difficulty
**Hard.** Build this last. Rigid manufactured geometry is exactly where video models wobble — panel gaps, badges, wheel spokes, door shut-lines.

## Gotchas
- **Never hold a static wide shot of the whole car.** Keep the camera moving at all times; a held wide is where the geometry falls apart.
- Wheels are the worst offender. Prompt "solid turbine-style wheel covers, no open spokes".
- No badges, no number plate, no brand marks — all HTML.
- Consider `minimax_h3` at 2K for the ignition beat specifically; it holds hard edges better.
- Generate scenes 1–3 as one clip and 4–6 as an extension; the whole arc in one 30s take degrades badly at the cladding beat.
