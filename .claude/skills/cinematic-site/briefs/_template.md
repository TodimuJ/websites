# Brief template

Copy this shape when the user's description matches no existing brief.

## Identity
- **slug** — kebab-case, becomes `sites/<slug>/`
- **Niche** —
- **Physics of transformation** — the ONE physical process the scroll shows
- **Brand name candidates** — two invented options
- **Palette** — 4-5 named colours with hex
- **Type direction** — heading / body pairing

## Scene beats
Number them. Each is one continuous camera action. Name the **blur-through** beat
explicitly — the steam, ink, smoke, liquid or defocus moment that hides the hardest
state change.

## Ending frame
The "you leave with it" shot, described precisely. This is the last thing the visitor
sees and the most important sentence in the brief.

## Products
3-6 SKUs with names and a price tier. **Prices are US dollars.** Write the band as
`$low–$high` — never `£`, `€` or any other currency. See the market rule in `SKILL.md`.

## Difficulty
Easy / Medium / Hard, and what specifically is hard.

## Gotchas
Where video models fail on this subject, and the mitigation.
