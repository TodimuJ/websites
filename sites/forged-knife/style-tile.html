Imagery direction

Near-black ground everywhere. One hard low key light, one narrow ember rim. Violent falloff into black — no fill, no studio softbox wash. Macro stays tack sharp at maximum magnification; blur never stands in for scale. Nothing in any generated frame carries text, engraving, kanji or a maker’s mark.
