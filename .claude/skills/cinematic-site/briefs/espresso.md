# Espresso — "Nine Bars"

## Identity
- **slug** `espresso`
- **Niche** Speciality coffee — beans, subscription, or café
- **Physics** Fluid dynamics under pressure; grind fracture; crema emulsion
- **Brand names** `NINE BAR` · `PUCK & PRESSURE`
- **Palette** `--ink #100C09` `--crema #C98F52` `--espresso #3A2318` `--steel #8C8E90` `--paper #F2EDE6`
- **Type** High-contrast serif headings (Canela/Playfair register) + clean grotesk body

## Scene beats
1. **Opening** — Inside the grinder. A single coffee cherry, macro, rotating in low warm light. Camera pushes in; the cherry becomes the raw bean.
2. **Fracture** — The bean shatters. Grounds avalanche toward the lens in slow motion, filling the lower frame. Camera holds as the cloud settles.
3. **Compression** — Grounds settle into a puck. The tamp descends from above like a piston, lit from one side. Camera at puck level, extreme macro.
4. **BLUR-THROUGH — inside the puck** — Camera passes *through* the coffee bed as pressurised water is forced in. Dark rivulets, saturated grounds, everything liquid and backlit. This is where the hardest state change hides.
5. **Extraction** — Emerge below the basket. Two honey-thick streams braid and fall into a warm ceramic cup. Slow, viscous, sharp.
6. **Resolution** — Pull back. Crema swirls into tiger-mottle. Steam rises. The cup sits on marble under a single warm lamp; the room falls out of focus behind it.

## Ending frame
The full cup on marble, steam catching the rim light, crema pattern legible, negative space above-left for the closing headline and CTA.

## Products
Single-origin bag · seasonal blend bag · 3-bag subscription · ceramic tulip cup · tamper. Accessible-premium tier ($14–$48).

## Difficulty
**Easy.** Best first build — macro liquid is where video models are strongest and this will look expensive for the fewest generations.

## Gotchas
- Avoid a barista's hands mid-frame; models mangle fingers on portafilters. Keep hands out until the final lift, from behind.
- Say "crema remains sharp, no motion blur on the surface pattern" or extraction goes mushy.
- Latte art pours are high-risk — if the ending is a rosetta, generate it as its own extension and inspect before committing.
