# Lip gloss — "the gloss is the landscape"

## Identity
- **slug** `lip-gloss`
- **Niche** Colour cosmetics — gloss, shade range
- **Physics** Viscosity, surface tension, and a deliberate **scale deception**
- **Brand names** `GLOSSARIUM` · `SLICK & SALT`
- **Palette** `--wet-black #0B0708` `--nude #D7A98C` `--rose-gold #C98B6E` `--flesh #EFCDBB` `--shine #FFF6EF`
- **Type** Tight modern sans, wide tracking on the wordmark; no serif — this reads contemporary, not heritage

## Scene beats
1. **Opening (the deception)** — What appears to be a rose-gold desert dune at golden hour. Slow lateral drift across soft curved ridges. Reads completely as landscape.
2. **The reveal** — Camera pulls back steadily. The "dune" resolves into a single bead of gloss on a glass slide under studio light. Hold briefly on the recognition.
3. **Collapse** — Surface tension breaks. The bead collapses into a thick viscous ribbon that curls and folds in slow motion, shimmer particles catching light as it turns.
4. **BLUR-THROUGH — the pour** — The ribbon fills the frame entirely, translucent and backlit, everything gloss. Camera travels through it.
5. **Loading** — The ribbon threads itself onto a doe-foot applicator and retracts into the tube. The tube caps, rotates once, settles.
6. **Resolution** — Tight crop on a mouth. One press, transfer, lips part. End on the highlight catch-light.

## Ending frame
Close crop, one mouth, glossed, single specular highlight. Copy sits to the right in clean dark space.

## Products
6-shade range across nude / berry / clear / bronze, one hero shade named in the story. £18–£26.

## Difficulty
**Easy–Medium.** Second build. The scale reveal is the whole idea and it is cheap to achieve.

## Gotchas
- Lips are the one human feature these models render reliably — but keep it **one mouth, tight crop, no full face, no teeth**.
- The opening must not look like gloss. Prompt it as a landscape with no product language at all, then let the pull-back do the work.
- Shimmer particles need "fine metallic flake catching directional light" or they render as noise.
