# Mechanical keyboard — "the sound of a switch, seen"

## Identity
- **slug** `keyboard`
- **Niche** Enthusiast mechanical keyboards
- **Physics** Precision mechanism — spring compression, leaf contact, actuation
- **Brand names** `CLACK` · `TACTILE SUPPLY CO.`
- **Palette** `--anodised #3A3E44` `--pbt-cream #E4DED1` `--burnt #C2622C` `--deck #18191C` `--chrome #A7ADB4`
- **Type** Mono for headings (this audience reads mono as native), grotesk body

## Scene beats
1. **Opening** — A single keycap floating in void, rotating slowly, backlit so the legend-free top surface catches an edge light.
2. **Descent** — The keycap lowers onto a switch stem. Camera follows it down and **through** the top housing.
3. **BLUR-THROUGH — inside the switch (the hero shot)** — Interior of the housing at extreme macro. The stem legs bend the metal leaf aside; the contact closes; the spring compresses; a tiny blue spark at the moment of actuation. Nobody has rendered this shot — it is the reason to build this site.
4. **Multiplication** — Pull out. The switch seats into the plate, and switches cascade into place across the PCB like dominoes, row by row.
5. **Capping** — Keycaps rain down from above and land in rows. The milled aluminium case closes around the assembly, edges catching light.
6. **Resolution** — Hands typing at a low three-quarter angle. One keystroke sends a ripple of light across the board, which settles to steady backlight.

## Ending frame
The completed board at three-quarter view on a dark desk, backlight steady, one hand leaving frame. Copy right-hand side.

## Products
65% and TKL, assembled or barebones; switch options; two keycap colourways. $120–$340.

## Difficulty
**Medium–Hard.** The safer version of the EV lesson — rigid geometry, but small, dark and forgiving.

## Gotchas
- **No legends on keycaps.** Generate blank caps; models produce garbled glyphs every time. Blank caps also read as premium in this niche, so this is a free win.
- Hands: keep them to the final beat, low angle, partially out of frame.
- The interior switch shot needs "cutaway cross-section of the switch housing interior, dramatic macro lighting" — without "cutaway" you get an exterior view.
- Grid alignment drifts across the cascade beat. Generate 4–6 as an extension so the plate geometry is inherited rather than re-invented.
