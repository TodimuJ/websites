# Briefs

Ten scroll-story concepts, spread across **different physics of transformation** rather
than different product categories. That is the axis that decides whether a scroll-scrub
looks expensive or looks broken — liquid, fire, ice, growth, mechanism and refraction all
fail in different ways, so five builds across five physics teaches far more than five
builds across five industries.

| Brief | Niche | Physics | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| `espresso` | Speciality coffee | Fluid under pressure | Easy |
| `lip-gloss` | Cosmetics | Viscosity + scale deception | Easy–Med |
| `perfume` | Niche fragrance | Diffusion, smoke, refraction | Medium |
| `botanical-serum` | Skincare | Growth + double scale inversion | Medium |
| `vinyl` | Reissue label | Macro spiral, time reversal | Medium |
| `spirits` | Aged spirit | Ice, refraction, fracture | Medium |
| `forged-knife` | Kitchen tools | Material state change | Med–Hard |
| `keyboard` | Enthusiast hardware | Precision mechanism | Med–Hard |
| `satellite` | Connectivity | Extreme scale range | Med–Hard |
| `metal-card` | Private members' card | **Material transmutation** — fixed geometry | Med–Hard |
| `ev` | EV marque | Rigid mechanical assembly | Hard |

## Suggested build order

Optimised for learning, not for easy wins — five different failure modes:

1. **`espresso`** — get the pipeline working on the friendliest physics
2. **`lip-gloss`** — scale illusion, and the one safe human close-up
3. **`perfume`** — fully abstract morphing between unlike states
4. **`forged-knife`** — real material state changes, steam as a transition
5. **`keyboard`** or **`ev`** — rigid mechanical assembly, genuine hard mode

## Concepts without brief files

Designed but deliberately left unwritten — being tested by manual prompting instead:

- **Kintsugi ceramic** — a shatter played in reverse, cracks filling with gold. Generate the shatter *forward* and reverse it in ffmpeg rather than prompting implosion.
- **Honey** — camera inside a hex cell, wax capping, uncapping, the ribbon pour. Avoid photoreal bees.
- **Fountain pen** — macro ink meniscus, the capillary slit, the stroke laying onto paper. The ending is a trap: a legible signature is generated text. Draw it as inline SVG over a blank final frame instead.

Use `_template.md` for anything new.
