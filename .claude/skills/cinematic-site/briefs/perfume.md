# Perfume — "the note pyramid, inhaled"

## Identity
- **slug** `perfume`
- **Niche** Niche fragrance house
- **Physics** Ink-and-oil diffusion, smoke, glass refraction
- **Brand names** `NOIR ORPHÉE` · `MAISON VÉTIVER`
- **Palette** `--bone #EDE6DA` `--blush #D9A9A2` `--oxblood #5C1F22` `--charcoal #1A1719` `--gilt #B08A4F`
- **Type** High-contrast display serif, deco-leaning. Small caps for navigation.

## The conceptual hook
A fragrance's note pyramid is **literally vertical**. Top notes at the top of the page, base notes at the bottom. Label the scroll progress `TOP / HEART / BASE` — the scrollbar becomes the pyramid. This is the idea; protect it.

## Scene beats
1. **Opening** — Inside the bottle, looking out through frosted glass. Light bends and separates. Abstract, luminous, no product readable yet.
2. **BLUR-THROUGH — the bloom (TOP)** — A single drop falls into clear liquid and blooms in macro. Ink-in-water diffusion, tendrils unfurling. The bloom resolves into a bergamot peel spiralling open.
3. **Heart** — The citrus dissolves mid-rotation into rose petals, turning. Warm, saturated, still abstract enough to avoid botanical uncanny-valley.
4. **Base** — Petals darken and warm into oud resin. Smoke rises and thickens, taking the frame.
5. **Condensation** — The smoke gathers and *solidifies* into the bottle, standing on black stone. Camera settles.
6. **Resolution** — One spray plume releases and freezes into suspended gold particulate, held in a single beam of light.

## Ending frame
Bottle on black stone, frozen plume of gold particulate above and right. Deep negative space left for copy.

## Products
The hero EDP in 50ml and 100ml, a 10ml travel spray, a discovery set of 3. Luxury tier (£95–£240).

## Difficulty
**Medium.** Fully abstract morphing — no rigid geometry to betray the model. Forgiving to generate, hard to art-direct.

## Gotchas
- Do **not** attempt photoreal recognisable flowers. Keep petals abstract, backlit, partially out of frame.
- Label text on the bottle must not be generated. Render the bottle unlabelled and place the wordmark in HTML over it, or generate a blank cartouche.
- Smoke wants to become fog and flatten the frame — prompt "dense directional smoke with clear negative space above".
