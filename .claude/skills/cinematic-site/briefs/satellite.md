# Aerospace / connectivity — "the last 400 kilometres"

## Identity
- **slug** `satellite`
- **Niche** Satellite connectivity — consumer broadband or IoT backhaul
- **Physics** Extreme scale range (a bolt → low-earth orbit → a pocket) and mechanical deployment
- **Brand names** `MERIDIAN ORBITAL` · `ARC ONE`
- **Palette** `--vacuum #04060C` `--mli-gold #C9A227` `--ceramic #EDEFF2` `--earth-blue #2E6F9E` `--signal #5FE3C0`
- **Type** Precise technical sans, tight tracking. Mono for all figures. Generous whitespace.

## Scene beats
1. **Opening** — A polished machined aluminium bracket, macro, rotating in void. Deliberately unidentifiable — it could be anything.
2. **Assembly** — It bolts to a strut. Struts assemble into a frame. Panels fold in and the object becomes recognisably a satellite. Camera pulls back through the build.
3. **Deployment** — Solar arrays unfurl in slow accordion, gold MLI foil catching hard unfiltered sunlight.
4. **BLUR-THROUGH — the pull-back** — Camera accelerates backward. The satellite becomes a speck against Earth's terminator line, city lights glittering below. Then the Earth becomes a crescent, then a point of light in black. The frame is almost entirely dark at the apex.
5. **Return** — The camera falls back toward Earth, through the terminator, through cloud layers, atmosphere brightening.
6. **Resolution** — It lands on a phone held in a hand at dusk, on a hillside. The signal indicator fills.

## Ending frame
The phone in hand at dusk, screen lit, landscape soft behind. The product is connectivity and this frame is the only place it becomes concrete.

## Products
Consumer plans (3 tiers) plus a terminal. Or B2B — ask at Gate 1; it changes the whole copy register.

## Difficulty
**Medium–Hard.** Biggest scale range in the library. Space renders beautifully; the atmospheric re-entry beat is the risk.

## Gotchas
- Earth from orbit is very well-represented in training data — this beat will look great with little effort. Lean on it.
- The descent through cloud is the weak beat. Generate it as its own extension and expect two takes.
- The phone in hand: keep it a three-quarter rear view, screen glow only, no UI rendered. All screen content is HTML if shown at all.
- Solar array deployment wants to snap rather than unfold — prompt "slow continuous accordion unfolding, no cuts, panels articulating in sequence".
