# Botanical serum — "grown, not made"

## Identity
- **slug** `botanical-serum`
- **Niche** Botanical skincare, single-ingredient hero
- **Physics** Time-lapse growth, then two scale inversions (macro → cellular → macro)
- **Brand names** `TERRA LUMEN` · `SEED & SERUM`
- **Palette** `--loam #241E18` `--chlorophyll #4F6B3A` `--amber #C88B36` `--bone #F0EBE1` `--glass #C7D2C9`
- **Type** Humanist serif headings, low contrast; airy sans body. Generous line height.

## Scene beats
1. **Opening** — Dark wet soil in macro, lit from a low raking angle. A root threads down past the lens.
2. **Growth** — A green shoot pushes up through the frame, uncurling. Camera rises with it. Time-lapse cadence, continuous move.
3. **Leaf** — A leaf unfurls and fills the frame. Veins fill with transmitted light from behind.
4. **BLUR-THROUGH — into the leaf** — Camera pushes *through* the leaf surface: stomata, then cell walls, then a single glowing droplet of oil suspended inside a cell. The droplet grows until it floods the frame.
5. **Inversion** — Pull back out. The droplet is falling from a glass pipette against a bone-white ground. It lands on skin and spreads.
6. **Resolution** — Pull back to the amber bottle on a pale stone plinth, one leaf beside it, soft daylight from the left.

## Ending frame
Amber dropper bottle on stone, single leaf, daylight. The calmest ending in the library — copy centred below.

## Products
Hero serum 30ml · cleansing oil · barrier cream · the 3-step set. £34–£96.

## Difficulty
**Medium.** Two scale inversions instead of one, and growth time-lapse is well-represented in training data.

## Gotchas
- **Never a face.** Forearm, shoulder or collarbone only. Faces go uncanny and it destroys the calm.
- Cellular interiors want to look like stock microscopy — prompt "stylised luminous cross-section, cinematic, shallow depth of field, not scientific imagery".
- Bottle label: generate blank amber glass, overlay type in HTML.
- Keep the palette restrained. The temptation is over-saturated green; the brief's chlorophyll is deliberately muted.
